# Runtime screenshots

These screenshots were supplied by the project owner during prototype review.
The title and tactical-grid captures retain their complete visible image.
The original/enhanced pair is cropped from the two game windows in a desktop
capture, excluding the window titles and surrounding desktop. Cropping and PNG
encoding preserve the selected pixels; there is no generated or repainted art.

The two windows show separate sessions and camera positions. This comparison
illustrates presentation options, not frame synchronization or parity results.
Game logos, artwork and screenshots retain their original owners' rights.
`captures.json` records source hashes and crop rectangles without local paths.
